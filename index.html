Designing for the AI era

AI that keeps people in control.

AI agents, agentic workflows, conversational UX and human–AI interaction, designed to remove friction while staying clear and user-controlled. Pick an everyday moment to see the difference.

Without AI 5 steps

  1. Open the filters
  2. Pick a category
  3. Set a date range
  4. Choose a sort order
  5. Start over when it's wrong

With AI 1 step

You ask

YouShow unpaid invoices from last month.

AI answers

14 unpaid invoices from September

  • Sorted with the oldest first

Why it worksPeople ask in their own words instead of setting filters, and the screen shows what the AI understood so they can correct it.

Without AI 4 steps

  1. Open the 40-page report
  2. Read all of it
  3. Pull out the key points
  4. Copy them into a doc

With AI 1 step

You ask

YouSummarize the key risks.

AI answers

3 key risks found

  • Renewals dropped in two customer segments (p.4)
  • Support backlog is growing week over week (p.12)
  • One vendor contract expires next quarter (p.27)

Why it worksA short summary with page references, so people can check the source.

Without AI 4 steps

  1. Type your name and address
  2. Upload the same documents again
  3. Re-enter company details
  4. Check everything for typos

With AI 1 step

You ask

YouUse my details from the last application.

AI answers

9 of 12 fields filled in

  • Name, address and company details added
  • 3 fields need a quick check

Why it worksFill in what is already known and clearly mark what still needs a person to check.

Without AI 4 steps

  1. Read the setup guide
  2. Go through a 7-step wizard
  3. Configure each setting
  4. Invite the team one by one

With AI 1 step

You ask

YouSet this up for a 5-person design team.

AI answers

Workspace ready to review

  • 5 seats and roles set
  • 3 starter templates added
  • Invites drafted, not sent

Why it worksThe AI does the setup work, but nothing is sent until the person reviews it.
